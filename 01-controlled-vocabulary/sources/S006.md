## Can the other player mark an empty square after a win?

Source suitability reviewed on 2026-11-25 for the [legal-move question](../../README.md#first-competency-question-proposed).

**Source evidence.** Page 1, opening paragraph, states that play continues “until either” of two conditions occurs. In case (1), a player completes a row, column or diagonal with their own marks. In case (2), the ninth move fills the grid without either player completing a line. The final sentence identifies the outcomes as a win and a draw respectively. [Noughts-and-crosses, p. 1](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/barbeau/fn26.pdf#page=1)

**Interpretation.** No. Case (1) explicitly ends play as soon as the line is complete. Only case (2) requires a full grid. It follows that even if a square is empty after a win, the other player has no turn in which to mark it. [S005](S005.md) illustrates this consequence on a particular board. S006 states the rule for the paper-and-pencil game itself.
