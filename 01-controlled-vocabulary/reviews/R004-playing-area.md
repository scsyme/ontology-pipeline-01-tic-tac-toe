Scott selected [grid](../terms/T016.md) after considering [board](../terms/T017.md), the assistant’s recommendation. The grid definition awaits approval. Board remains a candidate for the alternative-term review later in Stage 1.

## Interpretation

In MIT’s placement instruction, substituting grid for playing board preserves which nine locations a player can choose from. A move changes the contents of one cell while the arrangement of cells persists. This supports considering the two labels together in this context. Formal assignment of an alternative term is a later Stage 1 decision.
