# cell

[Source record S006](../sources/S006.md)

Evidence reviewed on 2026-11-27. In the opening sentence, square modifies the shape of the grid. Later mentions of middle and corner cells qualify particular locations.

## Stakeholder decision

On 2026-11-27, Scott Syme selected cell as the preferred term for the individual board location considered in [R001](../reviews/R001-board-location.md). His instruction was “Proceed with cell”.

Use cell for this meaning in subsequent work on the conventional 3×3 game. The status preferred records this choice of wording. Scott approved the definition below on 2026-11-27.

[Square](T001.md), [space](T002.md) and [position](T004.md) remain candidates for later consideration during the thesaurus stage, including the ambiguity noted for position.

## Agreed definition

One of the nine locations in a conventional 3×3 tic-tac-toe board, each of which can hold a single X or O.

Scott Syme approved this wording on 2026-11-27 in response to the proposed definition. The occupancy terminology raised in the same response is considered in [R002](../reviews/R002-cell-occupancy.md).
