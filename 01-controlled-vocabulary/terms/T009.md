# symbol

Preferred general term for what a player places in a cell. See [R003](../reviews/R003-cell-content.md) for interpretation and coverage.

## Stakeholder decision

On 2026-11-27, Scott Syme approved symbol as the preferred term after reviewing the corpus comparison. In response to “Shall we adopt it as the preferred term?”, he replied “Yes”.

Use symbol for this meaning in subsequent work. Scott approved the definition below on 2026-11-27. Further treatment of the other candidate terms remains for later review.

## Agreed definition

An individual mark in a cell whose form, X or O, indicates which player marked that cell.

Scott Syme approved this wording on 2026-11-27 during the [form and occurrence review](../reviews/R003-cell-content.md#wording-approval).

## Earlier approved definition

An X or O placed in a cell to indicate which player marked that cell.

Scott Syme approved this wording on 2026-11-27. Asked whether this definition captured his intended meaning, he replied “Yes”.

Here, symbol refers to an individual occurrence in a cell. Several cells can contain symbols of the same form.
